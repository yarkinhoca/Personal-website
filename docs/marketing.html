Overview

CanoramIQ connects to your car's OBD-II port via a standard Bluetooth adapter and fuses 100 Hz CAN bus data with your phone's GPS and inertial sensors. The result is a complete picture of your vehicle's health, performance, and driver behaviour — updated in real time, computed entirely on your device.

Whether you're a weekend track driver chasing lap times, a daily commuter who wants to catch faults before they become expensive repairs, or a fleet manager tracking driver behaviour, CanoramIQ delivers data you can actually act on.

CanoramIQ is available for iOS and Android. No subscription is required for core OBD logging and diagnostics — advanced analytics are available as an optional upgrade.

Key Features

CanoramIQ packs professional-grade capabilities into an app anyone can use.

100 Hz OBD-II Streaming

Reads engine RPM, speed, throttle, MAF, coolant temp, fuel trim, and more at up to 100 frames per second.

GPS Route Mapping

Every drive session is overlaid on an interactive map with speed heat-maps and segment-level KPI breakdowns.

Predictive Fault Detection

On-device Isolation Forest and neural network models flag anomalies weeks before a DTC appears on your dashboard.

Live Physics Engine

Solves your vehicle's true road-load mass and aerodynamic drag coefficient in real time during each drive.

Driver Behaviour Score

IMU-fused braking intensity, cornering G-force, and throttle smoothness are combined into a single per-session score.

Drive History & Reports

Browse past sessions, compare KPI trends over time, and export drive reports as shareable PDFs.

On-Device AI

Runs locally — no cloud required

CanoramIQ's machine learning models run entirely on your phone's CPU and Neural Processing Unit (NPU). Your driving data never leaves your device as part of any inference pipeline.

  • Isolation Forest anomaly detector: Learns your specific engine's normal operating envelope over your first few drives, then flags statistical outliers in real time. False positives are minimised because the model is personalised to your vehicle, not a generic baseline.
  • Neural network diagnostics: A lightweight feed-forward network cross-references multi-parameter anomaly patterns against known failure signatures to suggest probable root causes — before a DTC is triggered.
  • Self-zeroing IMU platform: Gyroscope bias and accelerometer offsets are recalibrated automatically at the start of every drive, ensuring accurate G-force and inclination readings regardless of how the phone is mounted.

Because all AI inference is on-device, CanoramIQ works without an internet connection. Fly somewhere, rent a car, plug in — it just works.

Privacy First

We designed CanoramIQ from the ground up with privacy as a constraint, not an afterthought.

  • All vehicle telemetry, GPS tracks, and computed KPIs are stored in a sandboxed local database on your device.
  • No raw sensor data is transmitted to any external server.
  • Location access is requested only while the app is in the foreground during an active drive session.
  • You can delete all your data at any time from within the app — no support ticket required.
